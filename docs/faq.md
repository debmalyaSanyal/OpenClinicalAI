# FAQ

## Is OpenClinicalAI a chatbot?

No. It is a platform foundation for safe clinical AI modules.

## Does it include an OCR model?

No model is hardcoded. OCR engines are plugins.

## Does it generate medical advice?

No core module invents medical facts. Medical knowledge plugins must be evidence-backed and safety-validated.

## Why FHIR-first?

FHIR gives clinical data a standard shape that downstream systems can validate, exchange, and audit.
